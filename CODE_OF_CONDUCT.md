# Code of Conduct

The Training Grounds is built for the FPV community: pilots, race directors, clubs and anyone learning to fly. Everyone should feel welcome here.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to FPV or to GitHub.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you assume.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory jokes or language.
- Sharing someone's private information without permission.
- Spam or repeated off-topic posts.

## Enforcement

Comments, issues or pull requests that break these rules may be edited, hidden or removed, and repeat offenders may be blocked from the project.

To report a problem privately, use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment or issue, or contact the maintainer through the [GitHub profile](https://github.com/abhjz).

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
